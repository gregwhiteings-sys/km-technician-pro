C0202 — Tray 1 Lift-Up Failure

Error Type: Paper Feed / Tray Mechanism

Condition: Tray lift motor does not reach upper limit within specified time.

Causes: Obstruction, incorrect paper size, lift motor/sensor failure, mechanical bind, loose connector, failed lift clutch.

Repair: Pull tray → check obstructions → verify paper size → inspect lift wire/gears/clutch → reseat connectors → clear error → test.

🎬 Search YouTube

C0601 — Fuser Warm-Up Failure

Severity: 🔴 CRITICAL — Machine will not run.

Condition: Fusing unit does not reach operating temperature within time limit.

Causes: Fuser not seated, dirty/displaced thermistor, blown thermofuse, burnt heater element, low mains voltage, fuser motor failure.

⚠️ CAUTION: Let cool 15+ minutes before service. Do NOT repeatedly reset — fire risk.

Repair: Power off → reseat fuser → check connectors → inspect thermistor/thermofuse/heater → clear error → power cycle → test.

🎬 Search YouTube

C2550 — Black Developer Density Error

Error Type: Imaging / Developer Unit

Condition: Toner density sensor detects abnormal developer-to-toner ratio.

Causes: Dirty ID sensor, developer expired, incorrect toner concentration, developer unit not seated, wrong developer type, transfer belt contamination.

Repair: Clean ID sensor → check developer counter → reinstall developer → run Auto Density Adjustment → inspect transfer belt → test.

📄 Jam Codes & Paper Path

General Jam Clearing Procedure

1. Locate indicator → 2. Open correct door → 3. Remove paper GENTLY in direction of travel → 4. Check for scraps → 5. Inspect sensors/rollers/transfer roller → 6. Verify paper size → 7. Close & test.

Key Rule: Never pull paper backward — can damage sensors, transfer belt, and leave torn fragments causing repeat jams.

🔄 Transfer Belt Unit (Image Transfer Belt)

Function: Carries image from drums to paper — critical for colour registration & density.

Part Numbers:

• A161R71300 — bizhub C224e, C284e, C364e, C454e, C554e (ITB Unit)

• A7UUR70400 — bizhub C258, C308, C368, C458, C558, C658

• A227R72911 — bizhub C220, C280, C360 series

Life Expectancy: ~100K–200K copies (varies by model)

Symptoms when failing: Colour misalignment, streaks, belt scratch marks, ghosting, density differences, error codes C2201/C2202/C2301

⚠️ Important: Handle belt by edges only — do NOT touch surface with bare hands. Reset belt counter after replacement. Run registration & density calibration.

🟠 Transfer Roller / Corona Unit

Function: Applies electrical charge to pull toner image from drum or belt onto paper. Monochrome machines often use transfer roller; colour use transfer belt + secondary transfer roller.

Part Numbers:

• A50UR70100 — Secondary Transfer Roller Assembly C224e/C284e/C364e

• A7UUR70300 — Secondary Transfer Roller C258/C308/C368 series

• 4034R70900 — Transfer Roller (B&W series: 223/283/363/423)

Life Expectancy: ~100K–150K copies or when transfer bias becomes inconsistent

Symptoms when failing: Faded prints, image not transferring, speckles, background toner, one side faint, image loss on large paper, voltage-related errors

⚠️ Maintenance: Clean corona wire/grid with provided brush or lint-free cloth. Check bias contact points for toner buildup or oxidation.

⚙️ Clutches — Feed, Lift, Drive

Function: Electromagnetic clutch engages/disengages drive to specific components — paper feed, tray lift, duplex, bypass, developer rotation.

Common Types & Symptoms:

• Tray Feed Clutch: Paper not feeding from specific tray, double-feeds, no pickup — click but no movement. Located near paper feed motor or tray drive assembly.

• Tray Lift Clutch: Tray won't lift — motor runs but tray stays down, or lifts intermittently. Often part of lift motor gearbox or separate solenoid/clutch.

• Main Drive / Developer Clutch: Image intermittently missing, developer not turning, noise during rotation, density errors C2550.

• Duplex / Reverse Clutch: Duplex jams, one-sided only, paper not reversing, timing errors.

Testing: Listen for click when activated. Check voltage signal vs mechanical engagement. Clean contact points. Replace if slipping or noisy.

🎬 Clutch Repair Videos

⚡ Motors — Main, Fuser, Scanner, Lift, Fan

Function: Drive systems — power supply to mechanical motion. Most are stepper or DC brushless motors with home/position sensors.

Common Motors & Symptoms:

• Main Drive Motor: Machine hums but won't ready, error codes C0500/C0550, timing errors, no rotation at all. Check for locked gears first before replacing motor.

• Fuser / Fixing Motor: Fuser heater OK but no rotation, paper jams at exit, "please wait" forever, error C0603/C0604. Check drive gears & bushings.

• Tray Lift Motor: Tray won't rise, error C0202/C0204, motor runs but gear stripped, or dead silent. Often includes gearbox assembly — replace complete unit.

• Scanner / Exposure Motor: Lamp moves slowly or not at all, scan error, copy cut off, noise during warm-up, home position error. Lubricate rails first.

• Laser / Polygon Motor: No image, black copies, error C2201/C2301, long warm-up, loud high-pitched whine that doesn't stop.

• Cooling / Exhaust Fan Motor: Error C0500, overheating, machine shuts down mid-run. Clean dust first — motor may just be blocked.

⚠️ Testing: Check connector for 24V + signal. Listen for attempt to start. Run Service Mode Input/Output test to isolate motor vs control board vs mechanical bind.

🔍 Step-by-Step Diagnostic Workflow

Standard Service Procedure

1️⃣ IDENTIFY: Exact model & serial number

2️⃣ ERROR: Record full code + sub-code + when it occurs

3️⃣ SYMPTOM: Copy? Print? Scan? Fax? Warm-up? Specific tray? Colour only? Transfer belt area?

4️⃣ OBVIOUS: Paper, toner, doors, trays, power, environment, transfer belt surface, corona wires

5️⃣ POWER CYCLE: OFF → Wait 60 sec → ON → Does error return?

6️⃣ CONNECTORS: Reseat harnesses, boards, units (power OFF first) — belt connector, transfer bias connector, motor power plugs

7️⃣ COMPONENTS: Clean sensors, belt surface, coronas; check clutch operation; check motor run signal; check drive gear free movement

8️⃣ SERVICE MODE: Input/Output checks — activate motor/clutch individually, sensor readings, belt home position

9️⃣ REPAIR: Replace confirmed failed part → Reset counters → Clear error history → Calibrate (belt registration, density) → Verify → Document

🧰 Maintenance & PM Procedures

Preventive Maintenance Schedule

Every 50K copies / Monthly: Clean feed rollers, separation pads, paper path sensors, scanner glass, white reference plate, transfer roller/corona wire, belt edge seals

Every 100K copies: Replace feed rollers & separation pads, clean fuser guides, check developer counter, inspect transfer belt surface for scratches, lubricate tray lift rails

Every 200K copies: Inspect/replace transfer belt & transfer roller, fuser inspection/replacement, lubricate scanner rails, check clutch engagement timing, inspect motor drive gears

Every 500K copies: Full PM kit — developer, drum, transfer belt, fuser, rollers, clutches, main drive belt, fans/motors

🖼️ Print Quality Troubleshooting

Visual Symptom Guide

🔍 Light Print / Faded: Toner low → Density setting → Developer expired → ID sensor dirty → Laser window dusty → Transfer roller worn/charged poorly → Transfer belt contamination → Damp paper → Check clutch engaging properly

🔍 Dark Print / Background: Developer contaminated → Toner leak → Laser not turning off → Corona dirty → Wrong toner → High voltage issue → Transfer bias voltage incorrect

🔍 Vertical Lines / Streaks: Drum scratched → Cleaning blade damaged → Fuser roller mark → Laser glass dusty → Corona wire dirty → Transfer belt surface scratched → Roller contamination

🔍 Repeating Marks: Measure distance → Matches: Drum (~94mm), Developer roller, Fuser roller (~80mm), Transfer belt (~340mm) → Clean or replace that component

🔍 Ghosting / Image Offset: Fuser not hot enough → Pressure roller worn → Drum cleaning failure → Low mains voltage → Transfer belt cleaning blade failure → Fuser nearing end-of-life → Motor speed variation

🔍 Toner Not Fusing: Fuser temp too low → Thermistor displaced → Wrong paper weight → Damp paper → Fuser motor not rotating → Fuser pressure spring weak → Fuser counter expired
